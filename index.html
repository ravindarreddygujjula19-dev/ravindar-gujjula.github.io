THREAT INTEL

How I triage a suspicious email, step by step

Oct 17, 2026

[Sample post] A simple, repeatable process for checking headers, links and attachments before deciding what to do.

Phishing is still the most common way attackers get a first foothold. This is the simple process I follow when a suspicious email lands in my queue.

1. Check the headers

Compare the From, Reply-To and Return-Path fields. If they point to different domains, treat that as a red flag. Also check the SPF, DKIM and DMARC results.

2. Inspect links and attachments

  • Hover over links and compare the real address with the displayed text.
  • Never open attachments on your own machine. Use a sandbox.
  • Look up the domain age and reputation.

3. Decide and document

If it is malicious, block the sender and URLs, search for other recipients, and write down what you found so the next analyst can reuse it.

Good documentation turns one investigation into a playbook.
